\# Tesserae Manifesto



\*\*Structured inquiry for aspiring builders, young builders, and accessible audiences. No one thinks alone.\*\*



We believe:



\- Thinking is not just answering questions. It is clarifying, pushing back, distilling, reflecting, and traversing.

\- Access is not an add-on. It is the floor: screen reader, voice, keyboard, any language, any device.

\- Language is a skin. English is not the source of truth. IDs are.

\- Models are assistants, not authorities. Orchestrate them. Do not depend on them.

\- Safety is a fence, not a bouncer. CLM sets the boundary once. Then trust.

\- Determinism is trust. Onion makes every step replayable.

\- Community is the library. Plaintext pieces, language skins, domains — built by many, for many.

\- No one should be alone. Mycelial instances connect. Collaboration is native.



This is not a product. It is a scaffold. Build with us.
